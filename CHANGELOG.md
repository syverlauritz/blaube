# Changelog

Each release's notes on GitHub come from its section here (`.github/workflows/build.yml`).

## [Unreleased]

- Settings: Size goes smaller (down to 0.3), and a new Liveliness slider turns everything they get up to down to calm or up to twice as lively.

- The snack jar shows your 5-hour session again, not the week (the week only takes over once it's over 75% used).
- The cookies in the jar tumble about when you shake, tip or drop it.
- Shake a blob too hard and it turns green, swells up and barfs; blobs it lands on aren't pleased.
- A new app icon that looks like today's blobs.
- Swear at a blob and it's scared and sad; talk-box messages now count for its feelings too.
- Messages from the talk box reach Claude at its next step again, and survive Blaube restarting.

## [0.2.0] - 2026-10-27

The first release.

- A softbody blob on your desktop for every Claude Code session, and a little one for every subagent, showing at a glance whether it's working, needs you or is done.
- Talk to a session from its blob: the conversation, permission prompts, questions and plans, answered right there.
- Start new sessions from Blaube, no terminal needed, or pick up a past one.
- The snack jar shows your Claude usage limit as cookies.
- They babble what's in their bubbles in a made-up language, and sing together.
- Updates itself: on Windows it installs new versions when you quit; on a Mac it tells you a new version is out (installing it for you needs a signed build).
